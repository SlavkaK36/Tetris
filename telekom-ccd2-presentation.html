The Regulatory Landscape

CCD II: The biggest shake-up
in consumer credit since 2008.

2008 — CCD I

The Old World

Credit agreements under €200 exempt. Zero-interest deals largely exempt. Telco device installments operated in a grey zone. Disclosure was light, paperwork was minimal.

November 2023 — CCD II Enters into Force

Everything Changes

Directive 2023/2225/EU. Zero-interest credit, BNPL, and small-ticket deals all fall inside scope. The grey zone disappears. Slovak Telekom's installment plans are now formally consumer credit products.

November 2026 — Slovak Transposition Deadline

The Hard Stop

Member states must transpose into national law. Regulators will audit. Penalties can reach 4% of annual turnover. Non-compliance is not an option — but bad UX is equally catastrophic.

CCD II Decoded — What It Actually Requires

Five new rules.
One enormous opportunity to stand out.

📋

1 · Pre-Contractual Information (SECCI)

A standardised EU form must be provided before the customer signs. Includes APR, total cost, repayment schedule, right of withdrawal. Cannot be buried. Cannot be skipped.

🔍

2 · Creditworthiness Assessment

Creditors must assess ability to repay using reliable data. The assessment must be documented and retained. Even 0% 24-month phone deals qualify.

📊

3 · APR Disclosure — Even at 0%

Annual Percentage Rate must be calculated and prominently displayed. A 0% installment plan with a €5/month service fee still has an APR that must be shown.

↩️

4 · 14-Day Right of Withdrawal

Customers can cancel the credit agreement within 14 days with no penalty. The device must be returned or a lump-sum payment made. Process must be clear and simple.

💳

5 · Early Repayment Rights

Customers may repay early at any time. Compensation for creditor is capped at 0.5%–1% of early repaid amount. Must be prominently communicated.

⚠️

The Trap to Avoid

Translating these 5 rules into 5 extra clicks, 3 PDFs, and a 12-minute store experience is the fastest way to lose Mária to Alza. Compliance ≠ complexity.

Business Rules Redesign · Creditworthiness Assessment

The invisible credit check —
powerful behind the scenes, invisible up front.

CCD II Requirement

Creditors must assess the consumer's creditworthiness before concluding a credit agreement, using "relevant and accurate" information from the consumer or from databases — including credit bureaus. The assessment must be documented and capable of review by supervisory authorities.

ST's Data Advantage

Payment history (24 months) Contract tenure ARPU trend Bureau score (NBCB) Prior installment behaviour Subscriber segment

The 3-Tier Assessment Engine

🟢 Tier 1 — Green Light (auto-approve)

Existing ST subscriber · 18+ months good standing · Device value ≤ 3× monthly ARPU. Decision: instant, no bureau call. 70% of applicants.

🟡 Tier 2 — Enhanced Check

New customer or recent subscriber · Device value > 3× ARPU. External NBCB bureau call triggered automatically (8-second response). 25% of applicants.

🔴 Tier 3 — Decline or Partial

Failed bureau check. Offer smaller device, or offer SIM-only. Decline is documented with reason code. Customer receives compliant refusal notice. 5% of applicants.

Business Rules · APR Communication

"0.0% APR" is not a footnote.
It's your headline.

The Problem with "Fine-Print APR"

Most operators will comply with APR disclosure by adding it to contract appendices in 8pt font. This satisfies the letter of CCD II but misses a massive commercial opportunity. Worse, regulators are explicitly watching for "disclosure in obscure locations" and will audit aggressively.

⚠️ Regulatory Risk Scenario

National Bank of Slovakia audits 100 contracts. Finds APR not "prominently communicated." Issues remediation order. €2M fine possible. Press coverage damages brand trust.

The Winning Approach: APR Front-and-Centre

On device shelf labels

"€29/month · 24 months · 0.0% APR · You pay exactly €696. Nothing more."

On website product pages

APR badge displayed alongside price. Tooltip explains: "Annual Percentage Rate — our measure of total credit cost. Ours: zero."

In advertising

"We'll tell you the APR before you ask." Position as radical transparency — a differentiator vs. open market BNPL providers.

Business Rules · Early Repayment

Make paying off early feel
like a reward, not a process.

CCD II Rule on Early Repayment

Customers have the right to repay the credit at any time, in full or in part. Compensation to the creditor is limited to 0.5% of early repaid amount (if >1 year remaining) or 1% (if <1 year remaining). Must be communicated clearly at contract signing.

What NOT to do

Hide early repayment in contract clause 14b. Make customers call a premium-rate number. Imply there are large penalties when there are not.
